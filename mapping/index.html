Map & learning sources

City markers use Natural Earth populated places, existing PDF city locations, and linked coordinates for a small number of Indian capital locations. Country cards show the available mapped cities; they are not complete city directories.

Region highlights use approximate Natural Earth physical label areas. These generalised outlines are not surveyed boundaries. Ocean currents use original schematic paths checked against NOAA’s major surface currents reference; arrows show usual flow and red / blue indicates warm / cold. Real current positions and strengths vary. Plate outlines: Peter Bird (2003), Hugo Ahlenius / Nordpil, PB2002 plate model, ODC Attribution 1.0. Plate outlines do not depict motion direction.

South America coverage follows your supplied give.pdf (pages 1–9), with approximate locations and original study notes. Broad regions are representative points; five river courses use Natural Earth geometry. Capitals: mledoze/countries (ODbL), with administrative-seat clarifications. Country and regional boundaries use Natural Earth. Coordinates: Wikipedia location snapshots; regional markers are approximate. India capitals include current names and seasonal seats.

Tributary courses: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Some records show mapped segments rather than complete courses; coverage is stated in the river notes. River-system references: CWC–ISRO River Basin Atlas ↗. Parent links show drainage relationships, not shaded basin areas.

State/UT outlines: geoBoundaries / DataMeet India community / Election Commission of India, CC BY 2.5 IN. Boundary metadata: updated in 2023; outlines are generalised. State boundary source ↗. Peaks and passes use approximate coordinates linked in each place’s notes; ranges use representative markers rather than surveyed extent polygons. Physical geography reference ↗.

River routes: Natural Earth 1:10m rivers and lake centre-lines. Context basemap: Natural Earth 1:50m countries. These are public-domain, generalised datasets; international boundaries reflect this dataset and are not an official Indian boundary map. Drainage groups filter river routes; shaded basin polygons are not included.
